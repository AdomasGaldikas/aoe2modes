Persian and Roman Latin voice conditioning use an original synthetic VoxCPM2 male voice.
The source model and inference code are Apache-2.0; the preserved license is in
licenses/VOXCPM-REFERENCE-CODE-LICENSE.txt. Per-clip references and revisions
remain in manifest.json. No stock game voice was extracted.

These copies preserve the original five civilization sets and extend them with

WAV files and their .wem aliases contain identical mono 48 kHz PCM16 audio; the
aliases are not Wwise-compressed WEM files. Native pronunciation, perceived
gender, and fresh-match playback still require listening/gameplay acceptance.
